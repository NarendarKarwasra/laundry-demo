Frequently Asked Questions About Best Dry Cleaning Service in India

Everything you need to know about pickup, pricing and garment care. Can't find your answer? Message us and we'll help.

What is dry cleaning?
Dry cleaning is a professional garment-cleaning process designed for eligible clothing that may require specialized cleaning rather than conventional home washing. The applicable process depends on the garment and its care requirements.
What clothes can be dry cleaned?
Eligible garments may include suits, blazers, jackets, coats, sarees, ethnic wear, dresses, formal clothing, and selected delicate garments. Acceptance depends on the fabric, construction, care label, and applicable cleaning requirements.
Is dry cleaning suitable for delicate clothes?
Some delicate garments can be professionally dry cleaned, but suitability depends on the fabric, garment construction, care instructions, and cleaning requirements. Garments should be assessed before processing.
Does dry cleaning remove stains?
Professional cleaning can help address many types of stains, but results vary depending on the stain type, fabric, age of the stain, and previous treatment. Some stains may not be completely removable.
How does CleanD's dry cleaning pickup work?
You select the required service and schedule a pickup. CleanD collects your eligible garments, processes them according to applicable requirements, and delivers the completed order back to your selected location.
Do you provide doorstep dry cleaning pickup and delivery?
Yes, doorstep pickup and delivery are available in eligible service areas, subject to service availability and the selected schedule.
How long does dry cleaning take?
Turnaround time can vary depending on the garment type, cleaning requirements, order quantity, location, and selected service. The applicable turnaround can be confirmed when placing your order.
How much does dry cleaning cost?
Pricing depends on the garment, service selected, quantity, and any additional requirements. Check CleanD's current pricing for the applicable rates.
Can I schedule dry cleaning pickup online?
Yes, customers can choose the applicable service, check availability, and schedule a convenient pickup online where CleanD's service is available.
Is dry cleaning better than regular washing?
Dry cleaning and regular washing serve different garment-care needs. Some garments are suitable for conventional washing, while others may require specialized cleaning based on their fabric, construction, and care label.
Can I send suits and formal wear for dry cleaning?
Eligible suits, blazers, trousers, and other formal garments can be submitted for professional dry cleaning, subject to their care requirements and service eligibility.
Can sarees and ethnic wear be dry cleaned?
Selected sarees and ethnic garments may be accepted for dry cleaning depending on their fabric, embellishments, construction, and care instructions.
